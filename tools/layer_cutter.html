Tool

Two fingers (or mouse-wheel) to zoom & pan. The coloured overlay shows what's selected. Switch the wand to ✖ removes to un-select a wrong flood — or just ↩︎/↪︎ in the top bar (⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z) to undo/redo any step. Pivot = the joint the part rotates around.

Clean up the selection

Knock-out white flood-fills the connected white background and keeps everything else — the fast first cut. Then Brush/Erase to fix, Grow to catch the soft pencil edge, Shrink/Feather to tidy.

Layers (back → front)

base = the body that stays joined. mover = a part you'll animate; tick “punch” so its shape is cut out of the base (no double image). Set each mover's 🎯 pivot at its joint.

Export

Send to app hands the layers + rig straight to the app over nexum (I bake the moving puppet, no files to pass around). Export .zip is the manual route: one transparent PNG per layer, the base with mover-holes punched, and rig.json.
